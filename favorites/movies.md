My Favorite Movies

Here are five of my favorite movies:

1. Avengers: Endgame

A superhero movie where the Avengers work together to defeat Thanos and save the universe.

2. Titanic

A romantic drama about two people who fall in love aboard the famous Titanic ship.

3. Harry Potter

A fantasy movie about a young wizard who discovers his magical abilities and attends Hogwarts.

4. Spider-Man: No Way Home

A superhero movie about Spider-Man facing challenges involving multiple versions of himself.

5. The Conjuring

A horror movie about paranormal investigators who help a family experiencing supernatural events.

These are some of my favorite movies because they have exciting stories and unforgettable characters.
